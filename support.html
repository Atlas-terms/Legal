Atlas Support

Help with Atlas — your travel history, on your device.

Questions, problems or suggestions:

atlas.appstore.application@gmail.com

Replies usually within a couple of days. Atlas is made by one person.

Reporting a problem

Atlas can produce a diagnostic report that makes a problem far easier to find. Open Settings → Privacy Centre → Diagnostics, review what it contains, and share it with your message.

The report holds counts, settings and the app’s warning trail — it is written to avoid your travel details, names and booking references. Read it before sending; nothing is sent automatically.

Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone. Atlas has no account and no developer server. If you turn on iCloud sync, a copy also goes to your own private iCloud account, which we cannot read. See the Privacy Policy.

I turned on iCloud sync and some items “couldn’t sync”

Check that you are signed into iCloud and that iCloud Drive is on, then use Sync Now. If a count persists, email us with a diagnostic report — that message means specific records were refused, and the report helps identify which.

Atlas read something from my ticket incorrectly

Every imported field can be corrected before it is saved, and edited afterwards from the record itself. Atlas shows the text it read for each field, so you can check it against the document. Nothing is saved without your confirmation.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, at any time and free of charge. Settings → Import & Export exports your whole archive as JSON or CSV. This is deliberate — your archive should never be locked inside the app.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Privacy Centre → Delete All Data removes your archive from the device. If iCloud sync is on, the copy held in iCloud is removed separately, in Settings → iCloud.

What does Atlas Pro include?

Unlimited trips (the free version holds 10), PDF reports, Atlas keeping your travel calendar up to date, and sharing a trip with the people you travelled with. Everything else — importing, the map, Passport, Insights, share cards, export and iCloud sync — is free.

I subscribed on another device / reinstalled the app

Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.

How do I cancel a subscription?

In Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Deleting Atlas does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime Pro never renews, so there is nothing to cancel.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Nothing is deleted or hidden. You keep viewing, correcting, exporting, restoring and deleting everything, iCloud sync keeps working, and trips beyond the free limit stay fully usable. Only creating new trips beyond the limit, new PDF reports, further calendar updates, and starting new shares stop until you subscribe again. The full detail is in the Terms of Use.

Someone shared a trip with me and I can’t open it

Viewing a trip somebody shared with you is part of Atlas Pro. Your invitation is held and waits — it is not lost, and there is no time limit. Once you have Pro, accept it and the trip appears.

Does Atlas work offline?

Yes. Reading documents, every figure, the Passport and Insights all work with no connection. Map tiles and iCloud sync need one.
