7 · Sharing a trip

Sharing a trip places a copy of it in your own iCloud account and gives the people you name read-only access. Atlas has no server and cannot see it. Both you and the person receiving it need Atlas Pro.

You choose, per trip, whether prices and booking references, your notes, and the text read from your documents are included. All three start off, and your imported original documents are never included.

If you stop sharing, the people you shared with keep the copy they already have. Updates stop; the copy does not disappear. Only deleting the trip removes their copy, and only once their device next connects.

You are responsible for what you choose to share, and for having the right to share information about other people that a trip contains.
